# Design

## Style Prompt

Editorial technical motion system: deep ink canvas, warm off-white typography, one electric lime signal color, rounded structural paths, and a calm forward camera feel. The design should make information feel like a living system resolving into order rather than a deck of cards arriving one after another.

## Colors

- `ink`: `#10151D` — primary canvas.
- `paper`: `#F4F0E8` — primary text and structural paths.
- `signal`: `#C9FF3D` — key change and proof state.
- `muted`: `#9AA5B5` — supporting labels.

## Typography

- Display: `Space Grotesk`.
- Supporting: `IBM Plex Mono`.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use generic blue gradients, tiny dashboard cards, or a persistent neon grid.
- Do not replace the transformation with a static before/after layout.
- Do not use more than one accent color.
- Do not let labels compete with the main moving object.
