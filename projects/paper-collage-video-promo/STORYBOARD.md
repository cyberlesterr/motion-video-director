# Storyboard — Paper Collage Video Pipeline

## Beat 01 · 0.0–4.4 · The real problem

The first frame is a warm paper desk with no product UI. A blue torn moon, a leaf-green scrap, a saffron tab, and a vermilion dot fall into an imperfect orbit. The headline assembles as scraps stop moving: “想做纸片动画，难的不只是出一张图。” Small labels identify the work that a still image cannot solve: `故事 / 分层 / 节奏`.

## Beat 02 · 4.4–9.6 · Intent becomes a system

The headline slides upward beneath a wide ink strip. Three large, offset paper panels enter from distinct directions. They do not become cards in a grid: their tabs physically meet and lock. Labels read `storyboard` → `assets` → `composition`. A line of ink underneath resolves into the phrase “可编辑的制作协议”.

## Beat 03 · 9.6–15.0 · The human stays in charge

The locked workbench folds into three angled planes. A vermilion stamp lands on each plane in sequence: `概念与档位` / `风格与试听` / `预览与修改`. The final stamp has the quiet secondary label `YOU DECIDE`. The three stamps converge along a single, correctly anchored vermilion thread.

## Beat 04 · 15.0–21.8 · A visible proof of relationship

A small nested tree on the left reads `scene` → `boat` → `character`. It contracts into a paper landscape on the right: water stays behind the boat; the character rests on the boat; the visual order is labelled with a thin proof thread that starts at the character tag and ends at the boat tag. The copy says the system handles rhythm, keyframes, and acceptance, but relationships must be proved.

## Beat 05 · 21.8–27.0 · A production line, not a pretty image

Paper elements build a final frame around the project title. The actual captured README appears only as a small perspective-cut provenance tile. The close uses the project address as a physical line of type and holds the full system for the final half-second. No source-brand clone, repo texture, generated image, generated video, or source video is used.
