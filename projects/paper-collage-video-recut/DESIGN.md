# Design System — Paper Collage Video Recut

## Overview

This is a 30-second, 9:16 Chinese product film for `paper-collage-video`.
It is not a GitHub walkthrough and it is not a deck of “feature” cards. The
film treats production as a physical paper world: a story must be built,
decisions have weight, layer relationships can be inspected, and the final
artifact can be opened and continued.

## Style Prompt

Editorial paper cinema. Work on a warm uncoated stock with midnight-blue ink,
vermilion decision marks, saffron construction paper, and deep leaf-green
stage pieces. The camera approaches a small number of tactile hero objects
with clarity: loose paper becomes a standing world; a human stamp changes the
state of the work; a proof line literally begins and ends on the objects it
explains. Every shadow describes a real overlap, fold, or height difference.

## Colors

- `stock`: `#F4EDDD` — main uncoated paper field.
- `ink`: `#142B47` — editorial blue-black, shadows, and the night table.
- `vermilion`: `#D7533C` — human decisions and irreversible emphasis.
- `saffron`: `#EAB64A` — construction layer and motion energy.
- `leaf`: `#6F8F79` — supporting scene depth and verified structure.
- `pulp`: `#CFC1A5` — stitch marks, quiet rules, and paper edge variation.

## Typography

- Display: `Songti SC`, `STSong`, serif — printed, deliberate, and cinematic.
- Technical labels: `SF Mono`, `Menlo`, monospace — restrained evidence voice.
- Use one compact sentence per hero frame. Labels may identify a material or
  proof relation but may never become a UI dashboard.

## Light, camera, and material

- Compose each hero around one physical subject; let the rest become framing
  evidence rather than equal-weight decoration.
- Use a top-down table only for construction. Use a shallow side angle for
  material height, a locked reading view for proof, and one controlled pullback
  for the final artifact.
- Paper is allowed to fold, stand, pin, press, mask, or cast a shadow. It may
  not drift continuously like a particle system.
- One emphasized object may receive a high-contrast highlight. Do not add a
  glint to every element.

## What Not To Do

- Do not reuse the previous title → cards → paper wipe rhythm.
- Do not make every scene a centered headline plus explanatory chips.
- Do not draw a line unless its two endpoints are visible and meaningful.
- Do not hide the project evidence in a tiny decorative screenshot.
- Do not use generic AI sparkles, neon gradients, card grids, fake GitHub UI,
  or a detached final URL slate.
