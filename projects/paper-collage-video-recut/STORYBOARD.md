# Storyboard — Paper Collage Video Recut

This is a pre-composition production package. It deliberately stops before a
full HyperFrames composition: the five styleframes must be reviewed before the
project can enter build work.

| # | Time | Shot family | One visual verb | Main subject | Evidence / transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | 0.0–4.0 | `subject-reveal` | **gather** | one unfinished paper scene | Loose cutouts pull toward the boat silhouette; the yellow timing strip becomes the horizon in shot 02. |
| 02 | 4.0–9.6 | `world-build` | **stand** | the paper world | Flat water, mountain, and scene pieces rise from their folds; the seam becomes the red decision thread in shot 03. |
| 03 | 9.6–15.0 | `decision-impact` | **press** | one human approval stamp | The stamp pins the production state; its circular edge is the camera portal into the relation proof. |
| 04 | 15.0–21.8 | `relationship-proof` | **prove** | boat–character relationship | A red proof thread begins on the character and terminates on the boat; depth layers turn just enough to reveal their order. |
| 05 | 21.8–30.0 | `artifact-encounter` | **open** | the real project artifact | A physical project folio opens from the proof frame; the captured public README is readable inside it and the address settles as a printed spine. |

## Energy arc

The film begins quiet and curious, becomes spatial in shot 02, lands its one
strongest impact in shot 03, slows down for legible technical proof in shot 04,
and ends on a proud artifact encounter rather than a CTA card. Each landing
gets a real settle: 0.6s after the first two actions, 0.8s after the stamp,
1.0s for the proof reading state, and 1.2s for the project identity.

## Visual rules to protect during implementation

- Shot 01 has one hero: the unfinished scene. Its supporting scraps never
  enter as a symmetrical card group.
- Shot 02 uses paper mechanics, not generic 3D UI. The subject rises from a
  shared hinge and has a grounded shadow.
- Shot 03 contains one decisive press event; the other production choices are
  material witnesses, not three equal informational cards.
- Shot 04 may use only one proof line. Its endpoints remain visible throughout
  the hero state.
- Shot 05 gives the authentic artifact enough area to read; it is not a tiny
  provenance badge. The final address is part of the folio spine, not a
  separate end card.
