# Script — Paper Collage Video Recut

The first build should use these as sparse on-screen anchors, not as a silent
slide narration. Voice and sound design are intentionally deferred until the
visual package is approved.

| Shot | On-screen anchor | Optional narration intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | `一张图，不是一条片。` | A still is only the beginning; a film needs a world that can move. |
| 02 | `故事 / 素材 / 构图，要站起来。` | The work becomes editable when its pieces have a physical relationship. |
| 03 | `人，不在流程外。` | The director’s decisions visibly change the production state. |
| 04 | `每一层，都要证明。` | A beautiful composition is not enough when the boat, character, and water have to stay correct. |
| 05 | `paper-collage-video` | The project opens as an artifact that can be inspected, changed, and continued. |
